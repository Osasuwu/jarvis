## Challenged assumption

**S7** — That removing `Confidence: N/100` removes self-assessed confidence from the design. The only mechanical consumer of /research's certainty is the label: `/dispatch` refuses any issue carrying a `needs-*` label (`skills/dispatch/SKILL.md:48-50`), and today removal keys on "confidence <50" (`skills/research/SKILL.md:250`). The proposal replaces that with "the main question has an exact answer that the remaining sub-questions cannot change" — a binary robustness judgement made by the same orchestrator, in the same pass as the reasoning, about its own answer; likewise "load-bearing claim", "sufficiency", and "quick lookup" are undefined self-classifications that decide whether the disconfirming search, the stop, and the audit happen at all. That is verbalized confidence relocated to the one place it has an effect, under the conditions the evidence base says make it worst (narrating confidence while reasoning, `03-methodology-evidence.md:119`), while the mitigation the same file records — estimating it in a separate call (`03-methodology-evidence.md:121`) — is given to claim verification only, not to the label decision or sub-question status, which the fresh-context auditor never rules on. The proposal treats the scalar's format as the problem; the evidence it cites is about who makes the judgement and when.
